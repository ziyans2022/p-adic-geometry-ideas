## 7. p-adic Simpson Correspondence

### Foundational

- Faltings, G. — *A p-adic Simpson correspondence*, Adv. Math. 198 (2005). The original construction for curves; introduces “generalized representations.”
- 🔥 Abbes, A., Gros, M. & Tsuji, T. — *The p-adic Simpson Correspondence*, Annals of Math. Studies 193, Princeton UP (2016). The definitive reference; two independent approaches (Abbes–Gros via period rings, Tsuji via crystalline topos). 603 pp.
- Liu, R. & Zhu, X. — *Rigidity and a Riemann–Hilbert correspondence for p-adic local systems*, Invent. Math. 207 (2017). Constructs the Higgs functor on the pro-étale site; foundational for modern approaches.

### Ben Heuer — Core Papers

- 🔥 Heuer, B. — *A geometric p-adic Simpson correspondence in rank one*, Compos. Math. 160 (2024), no. 7, 1433–1466. Geometrizes rank-1 correspondence via moduli of v-line bundles; characterizes essential image.
- 🔥 Heuer, B. — *A p-adic Simpson correspondence for smooth proper rigid varieties*, arXiv:2307.01303 (2023, v3: 2025). Proves the full Simpson equivalence $\text{Vect}(X_{\text{proét}}) \simeq \text{Higgs}(X)$ in all ranks and dimensions.
- 🔥 Heuer, B. — *Moduli spaces in p-adic non-abelian Hodge theory*, J. Algebraic Geom. (to appear), arXiv:2207.13819. Introduces smoothoid spaces; moduli-theoretic framework for the Simpson correspondence for any rigid group $G$.
- Heuer, B. — *Line bundles on rigid spaces in the v-topology*, Forum Math. Sigma 10 (2022). Studies v-Picard functors; key tool for rank-1 Simpson.
- Heuer, B. — *Diamantine Picard functors of rigid spaces*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. (to appear). Diamantine refinement of Picard functors; needed for the non-proper case.
- Heuer, B. — *The relative Hodge–Tate spectral sequence for rigid analytic spaces*, arXiv:2402.00842 (2024). Relative version of the Hodge–Tate decomposition for smooth proper families using smoothoid spaces.

### Ben Heuer — Collaborative Papers

- 🔥 Heuer, B. & Xu, D. — *p-adic non-abelian Hodge theory for curves via moduli stacks*, arXiv:2402.01365 (2024). For any reductive $G$ and a smooth projective curve $X/\mathbb{C}_p$: the moduli stack of $G$-Higgs bundles is a canonical twist of the moduli stack of v-topological $G$-bundles. Geometrizes Faltings’ correspondence.
- 🔥 Anschütz, J., Heuer, B. & Le Bras, A.-C. — *Hodge–Tate stacks and non-abelian p-adic Hodge theory of v-perfect complexes on rigid spaces*, J. reine angew. Math. (Crelle) 820 (2025), 235–305, arXiv:2302.12747. Constructs the derived p-adic Simpson functor via the Hodge–Tate stack; fully faithful embedding $\text{Perf}(X_{\text{HT}})^{\text{isog}} \hookrightarrow \text{Perf}(X_v)$.
- 🔥 Anschütz, J., Heuer, B. & Le Bras, A.-C. — *v-vector bundles on p-adic fields and Sen theory via the Hodge–Tate stack*, arXiv:2211.08470 (2022, v3: 2025). Describes $G_K$-representations via vector bundles on the Hodge–Tate locus of the Cartier–Witt stack; geometric perspective on Sen theory.
- Anschütz, J., Heuer, B. & Le Bras, A.-C. — *The small p-adic Simpson correspondence in terms of moduli spaces*, Math. Res. Lett. (to appear), arXiv. Connects AGT’s “small” correspondence to Heuer’s moduli-theoretic approach.
- Heuer, B., Werner, A. & Zhang, M. — *p-adic Simpson correspondences for principal bundles in abelian settings*, Canad. J. Math. (2025). Extends the Simpson correspondence to commutative rigid group $G$ in any dimension; characterizes when it exists via surjectivity of Lie group logarithm.
- Heuer, B. — *The p-adic Corlette–Simpson correspondence for abeloids* (with L. Mann, A. Werner), Math. Ann. (2022). For abeloid varieties: equivalence between finite-dimensional continuous representations of the Tate module and a subcategory of Higgs bundles.

### Prismatic Approach

- 🔥 Min, Y. & Wang, Y. — *p-adic Simpson correspondence via prismatic crystals*, arXiv:2201.08030. Proves rational Simpson equivalence via absolute prismatic site: Hodge–Tate crystals $\simeq$ enhanced Higgs bundles.
- Wang, Y. — *A p-adic Simpson correspondence for rigid analytic varieties*, arXiv:2101.00263 (2021). Liu–Zhu-style correspondence for varieties with liftable good reduction; compatible with AGT.

### Related: Ogus–Vologodsky and Characteristic p

- Ogus, A. & Vologodsky, V. — *Nonabelian Hodge theory in characteristic p*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 106 (2007). The characteristic-$p$ analogue of Simpson; Frobenius twist plays the role of the exponential. Useful analogy for p-adic intuition.
